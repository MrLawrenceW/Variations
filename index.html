Variation Formulas 變分公式

  • Direct variation: \(y = kx\)
  • Inverse variation: \(y = \dfrac{k}{x}\)
  • Joint variation: \(z = kx^{a}y^{b}\)
  • \(z \propto x\) and \(z \propto \frac{1}{y}\): \(z = \dfrac{kx}{y}\)
  • Partial variation: \(y = c + ax^{n}\) or \(y = ax^{p} + bx^{q}\)
  • Find k (isolate it): \(k = \dfrac{zy^{b}}{x^{a}}\)
  • % change of z: \((1 \pm p\%)^{a}(1 \mp q\%)^{b}\)
